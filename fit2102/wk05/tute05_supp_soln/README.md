## Approach for the exercises

This week's exercises are intended to build upon each other, culminating in the final implementation of a controllable rectangle screensaver. Please follow the instructions carefully.

It is highly recommended to **not** continue to later exercises without completing the previous exercises. If you are stuck, please refer to the hints or ask your tutor for help.

In this process, you may discover interesting patterns or issues with your previous implementations. If these do not impact the quality of the implementation for that particular set of requirements, it is not necessary to perform refactoring, though it could be a good exercise.
